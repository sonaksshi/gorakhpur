About

I started this project as a birthday present for my boyfriend. As he transitions to a new phase in life and plans to build a future outside his hometown, I wanted to create an archive of the city where he was born.
As life becomes concentrated in the country's metropolitan centers, very little is remembered of the places we leave behind. More so when you belong to cities that mainstream news, media, research, and pop culture routinely fail to etch into public memory. So much life is lived in these "small cities" of our country, yet so little of it is cohesively documented, and even less is truly understood.
A special mention to Omair Ahmed's piece "Small Town of No Significance" on the city of Gorakhpur. Although written seventeen years ago, it aptly captures the essence of this project.
As we move ahead in our lives, I hope to develop this into an evolving project that brings us closer to the cities that have shaped who we are, while making these places visible to the rest of the world.

All sources linked in this project will be archived on the Internet Archive to make sure nothing is lost to public indifference, political shifts, or simply the fragility of a buggy internet.
